**The idea.** India's crowded places, events, hospitals, metro stations, toll roads, run on people shouting into radios. MassFlow would be a company that sells order: sensors and cameras, edge compute, an AI command core and trained operators, sold as a service to the venue.

**What I did.** A concept brief and one day on a website that publishes scenarios to build credibility before any product exists.

**Why it is still an idea.** Capital-heavy, regulated, and it needs a first pilot partner. Written down here so it can be picked up or dropped on purpose.
