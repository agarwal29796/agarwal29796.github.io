**The idea.** Furniture and other visual-first brands lose sales because a customer cannot picture the product in their space. ArBoost gives a brand one embeddable script and a hosted showroom: a 3D viewer, a "view in your room" AR mode through a QR code, and cinematic renders from a single 3D model.

**What I built.** A showroom web app, a JavaScript SDK, and a small serverless backend on AWS that only signs URLs, with releases driven by git tags. The whole pipeline stood up in January 2026 and the SDK reached full AR-on-phone by the end of the month.

**What happened.** Outreach with a friend handling growth. Demos worked, no paying client. Last deploy in March 2026.

**Next.** Deciding between a pivot toward made-to-order furniture and kitchen makers, where the expensive problem is a customer approving a 2D drawing and then not recognising the result, or archiving it cleanly.
