**The problem.** A CA firm absorbs the cost of typing sales invoices, purchase bills and bank statements into filing and accounting software. The work is repetitive and the errors are expensive.

**What I built.** Two tools with a friend who runs a practice: one that fills a GSTR-1 return from the sales documents, and one that reconciles purchase-side input tax credit. Both run on AWS Lambda.

**What happened.** In use by one firm for feedback. No paying customer yet.
