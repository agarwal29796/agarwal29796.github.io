**The idea.** Recording a technical lesson on a general-purpose whiteboard is clumsy. TeachBoard is built for exactly that: draw a flow diagram, drop a table, annotate freehand, keep the UI out of the recording.

**What I built.** A React and TypeScript app with keyboard-first tools: select, pan, pencil with pressure, lines, arrows, shapes, text.

**What happened.** Live at teachboard.app since mid 2026. Grew out of the editor from Pulzr.
