**The idea.** Structured storytelling: give an LLM a prompt or a document, let agents pick slide templates and fill the slots, animate the result on a canvas with CSS motion, and render it in a headless browser.

**What I built.** A canvas editor with a timeline and path and text animation plugins, a prompt-to-frames prototype, a serverless render pipeline, a pitch deck and a waitlist page.

**What happened.** Archived in April 2026. The editor was the part people actually wanted, so it became [TeachBoard](https://teachboard.app).
